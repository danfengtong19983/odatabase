# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Everyone taking part in its issues, pull requests, discussions and other spaces is expected to
follow it.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer at danfeng.tong@gmail.com. Reports are handled
privately, and the reporter's identity is not shared without their consent.

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions
that don't follow the code of conduct, and may ban a participant temporarily or permanently, as the
Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
